How to play

Click one of your blue pieces, then click a highlighted square. Capture any enemy Hybrid to win.

Pieces
  • Hybrid: moves one square in any direction. If any Hybrid is captured, its player loses.
  • Mend: moves exactly two squares diagonally and cannot jump.
  • Con: moves to a square on the ring exactly two squares away.
  • Cond: moves on rings one, two, or three squares away and may jump over pieces.
  • Jem: combines Hybrid and Con movement. It may use Esso, but capturing it does not end the game.
  • Quin: moves like a Hybrid and may move twice in one turn. Each player has one Quin.
  • Monem: moves one square forward or right. It captures one square sideways or diagonally across any clear distance.
Special rules
  1. Esso: a Hybrid or Jem reaching the opposite edge duplicates into the nearest empty square on that edge.
  2. Esso stalemate: an enemy Hybrid directly beside it horizontally cancels Esso.
  3. Sequoia stalemate: an enemy Con orthogonally beside the original Hybrid prevents all of that player's Hybrids from moving.
  4. Permanent Sequoia: if the affected player has no other legal move, that player loses.
  5. Jem exception: a Jem beside an enemy Con is frozen until it has used Esso. After Esso, it is immune.
  6. Tema: when a player's only remaining piece is a Hybrid, it also gains the Mend's two-square diagonal move.

Current Esso placement: the duplicate appears in the closest empty square on the opposite edge.
